The heart of the country

A nation is made of its people.

From early-morning markets to quiet mountain villages, from lively streets to the calm coastline, Pakistan's spirit lives in its communities. Our ways of speaking, cooking, dressing, making music and welcoming guests may differ—but every community adds something meaningful to the national story.

“Pride of Pakistan” is a celebration of people rather than a competition between places. We honour the kindness of one neighbourhood, the creativity of another, and the resilience found in communities across the country.

Our special tribute

For the people of Quetta.

There is a special warmth in the name Quetta—a city framed by mountains, shaped by many stories, and carried in the hearts of people who call it home.

We celebrate Quetta not by placing other cities beneath it, but by giving its people a heartfelt spotlight of their own. The city's communities, traditions, hospitality and connection to the striking landscapes of Balochistan are an important part of Pakistan's rich, diverse identity.

A country in every direction

Four provinces.
A shared future.

Pakistan's regions hold distinct histories, landscapes and traditions. No short description can capture every community, but each deserves to be met with curiosity and respect.

⌁

Balochistan

Vast landscapes, rugged mountains, coastal communities, regional languages and deeply rooted traditions shape the province's diverse character.

Quetta · Gwadar · Turbat · Khuzdar · Chaman
❋

Sindh

From the energy of Karachi to the cultural heritage of Hyderabad and historic towns, Sindh carries a long tradition of art, commerce and poetry.

Karachi · Hyderabad · Sukkur · Larkana · Thatta
✺

Punjab

Fertile plains, historic cities, lively bazaars, music, literature and food traditions help give Punjab its unmistakable rhythm.

Lahore · Multan · Faisalabad · Rawalpindi · Bahawalpur
⌂

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Mountain valleys, historic routes, bustling towns and strong community traditions form part of the region's distinctive story.

Peshawar · Abbottabad · Mardan · Swat · Kohat
